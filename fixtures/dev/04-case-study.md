# Case study: a regional retailer

The retailer came to us with a slow, confusing website. Research suggests that slow pages lose visitors, and industry experts agree that clarity drives sales.

We rebuilt the navigation. Load time improved from 4.1 seconds to 1.9 seconds, measured on the retailer's own analytics export. Orders from the new product pages rose 18% over the following quarter.

There are pros and cons to a full rebuild versus a refresh, and it depends on your budget. Results may vary by market.

Overall, the project was a success.
