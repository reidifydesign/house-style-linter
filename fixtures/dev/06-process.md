# How a project runs

We start with a two-week audit. You get a written list of what is slowing your site down, ranked by cost.  Then you choose what we fix first.

Build runs in weekly cycles, 4–6 weeks in total. Every Friday you see a working build, not a slide deck.

The journey from audit to launch is short on purpose. We keep the journey honest by naming what we will not do.

Launch day is a checklist, not an event: DNS, redirects, analytics, and a rollback plan.
