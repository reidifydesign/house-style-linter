Choosing a content system can often be difficult for small teams, and it may take time. A good system could potentially save hours each week, although this might depend on your team. Some teams find that a simple setup usually works best in many cases. Others prefer a more complete tool, which can help when the workload grows. It is generally wise to start small and see what happens. The right choice tends to become clear after a few weeks of use. Perhaps a trial month would give you a better sense of the fit. Teams sometimes switch tools twice before they settle on one.
