# Notes from the test kitchen

We tested three doughs over two weeks. The slow-rise loaf beat the others on crumb and crust, and it kept for four days.

> "Give it a full day," said the baker. "The flavour is in the wait."

The overnight schedule:

1. Mix at 6 pm.
2. Fold at 9 pm.
3. Bake at 7 am.

Try it this weekend and send us a photo of your crumb.
